# Advice only for Metrics with moderate evidence

The first version produces Advice only for cadence, pelvic drop and vertical oscillation, the Metrics for which the literature shows at least moderate evidence of a link with injury or running economy. Overstride, knee flexion and tibia angle at initial contact, trunk lean and hip adduction are shown with their value, Typical Range and source, but without Advice; foot strike pattern and ground contact time are Descriptors. Systematic reviews find weak and inconsistent links between single kinematic variables and injury, and our 2D pipeline is not yet validated, so advising on weaker evidence risks presenting group averages as individual diagnoses. The list grows once the pipeline is validated against a reference.

Where the source gives speed-dependent ranges, the Typical Range depends on the Session's Treadmill Settings; outside the speed band covered by the source, the Metric has no Typical Range and therefore no Advice.
